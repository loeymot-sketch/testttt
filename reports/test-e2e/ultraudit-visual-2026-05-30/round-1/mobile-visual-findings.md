**No P0.** The app is visually coherent and on-brand (BLACK/ORANGE/YELLOW/WHITE) across every screen. Two real defect families:
1. **Image framing** (MV-01/02/03): a subset of product PNGs are shrunk-and-centred within their (transparent) frame so thumbs look unevenly filled; one landscape hero is dropped into a portrait box. Fixed **per image** (recrop/zoom source) — the rendering CSS (`object-fit:cover`, square boxes for square sources) is correct.
2. **Tall-recap quantity selector occlusion** (MV-13, **P1**): on a tall wizard recap (e.g. sandwich + 6 supplements) the "QUANTITÉ" stepper is **fully hidden behind the sticky "Ajouter au panier" CTA and cannot be scrolled into view** — measured `overlapsPx:50`, `isScrollable:false`. This is the owner's named "QUANTITÉ bar clipped behind sticky CTA" issue, **reproduced**. Needs a code fix (more bottom clearance / scroll room).

## ROOT-CAUSE NOTE (the headline)
The dominant, owner-visible issue (MV-01 / MV-02 / MV-03) is **NOT** a CSS/`object-fit` bug — `cover` + square boxes for square sources is correct. It is **source-image framing inconsistency**: a subset of product PNGs (drinks, frites cups, cheeses, desserts) are transparent-background with the subject shrunk-and-centred, while others (bols/tacos/burgers) are full-bleed; and one landscape hero (`cayenne-hero.png`) is dropped into a portrait box. **Fix = recrop/normalise the offending source images** (subject fills ~90% of a 1:1 frame; provide a portrait hero variant), which is a per-image asset task and does not touch protected/app code.
